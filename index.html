Rules
  • Fast 8, no slow rolling. Sit at 50g · Lvl 6 at 3-2 · 7 at 4-1 · 8 at 4-2 → roll down for 2★ Graves + Zac 📚 Carry's level
  • Items pick the carry: AD → Graves (preferred) · AP / Tear → Syndra, then Taliyah 📚 Two carries
  • Unlock as you go: 2 items on a Beach Pop unit → Ashe · Lvl 7 + 5 Beach Pop / Juggernaut → Zac · 1-to-5-cost line-up on Beach Pop hexes → Taliyah
  • Instruments touch your carries: Beach Pop units next to one get attack speed + mana; everyone next to one heals

🔓 Unlock path

Four of the core units start locked. Each unlock is a quest; once done, the unit shows up in your next shop and stays in your pool.

    🎸 Instruments

    • 🎯 Place them so a Beach Pop carry touches one: Graves + Taliyah share one, Syndra + Zoe the other
    • 🎲 Random pair each game: one of Rainstick / Maracas / Bongos + one of Panflute / Steeldrums / Ukelele
    • 🌊 Beach Pop hexes are what Taliyah's unlock counts: line up a 1-, 2-, 3-, 4- and 5-cost on them
    • 🔁 Move them like units between rounds when your carry moves

    Beach Pop 3 · 5 · 7

    • 🎵 Empowered = standing next to an instrument. Every empowered unit regenerates Health
    • 🏝️ Empowered Beach Pop units also gain Attack Speed + Mana, so put carries there first

            🧠 Advanced mechanics

            Plain answer first; open for the details.
            🎰 Shop odds + pool
            🔎 Copy finder · can I still 2★ Graves, and what will it cost?
            Expected copies per shop = 5 slots × that cost's odds × (copies left ÷ that cost's pool left). Averages: real rolls swing a lot.
            🎸 Instruments + Beach Pop hexes · carries next to an instrument
            • Instruments are placed like units. Move them every time your carries move, or the buff goes to the wrong unit
            • Non-Beach Pop units next to one (Yuumi, Illaoi) still get the Health regen, just not the attack speed + mana
            ✋ Holding units + contests · benched units are out of the pool
            • Every copy on any board or bench is out of the pool. A rival holding 2 Graves leaves 8 for everyone else
            • Scout before 4-2: if someone else is on Graves or Zac, roll at 8 anyway but plan for a 1★ in that slot
            • Holding a pair of a 4-cost for the roll-down is fine; holding pairs of units you'll sell is not
            👑 Beach Pop emblems · one fewer Beach Pop unit for 7
            • Beach Pop Crown = Emblem + Blue Buff + Syndra. Beach Pop Circlet = Emblem + Sparring Gloves + Graves
            • Put the Emblem on a unit you play anyway (Yuumi): Beach Pop 7 with a free slot for Swain or Illaoi
            ⬆️ When to go Lvl 9
            • Only after 2★ Graves + 2★ Zac, with HP 50+ and 50g+ left
            • Low HP? Spend at 8 instead: a stronger board now beats a better shop later
