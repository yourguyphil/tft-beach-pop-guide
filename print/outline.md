# Items explained
> What each core item does and why it's on that unit.

::: cols
::: box items
## {units:graves} Graves · AD
- {r:bt}{br}**Does:** Omnivamp + a shield at low HP.{br}**Why:** he dashes into fights, so he has to heal. **#1.**
- {r:sterak}{br}**Does:** AD + HP, big shield at 60% HP.{br}**Why:** keeps him alive through the first burst.
- {r:titans}{br}**Does:** stacks AD + resists when hitting or hit.{br}**Why:** he's in the middle of the fight, so it stacks fast.
- {r:qss}{br}**Why:** backup: CC immunity + attack speed.
:::
::: box items
## {units:zac} Zac · tank
- {r:gargoyle}{br}**Why:** Armor + MR per enemy targeting him.
- {r:warmog}{br}**Why:** his cast heals; more Health = bigger heal.
- {r:visage}{br}**Why:** heals more the lower he gets.
:::
|||
::: box items
## {units:syndra,taliyah} Syndra / Taliyah · AP
- {r:jg}{br}**Does:** AP + crit; abilities can crit.{br}**Why:** both deal all their damage with abilities. Syndra first, then Taliyah.
- {r:voidstaff}{br}**Does:** shreds Magic Resist.{br}**Why:** on Syndra; one shred is enough for the team.
- {r:shojin}{br}**Does:** attacks give bonus Mana.{br}**Why:** Taliyah casts every 40 Mana: more rocks.
- {r:rabadon}{br}**Why:** Taliyah's 3rd: raw AP.
- {r:bluebuff}{br}**Why:** Syndra's 3rd (tft.ninja board).

- Stop at ~10–20g once both are 2★

## When the plan breaks
::: cols
- **Bleeding early:** Lvl 6 at 3-2 and roll to ~30g for 2★ Beach Pop units
- **Graves contested:** roll at 8 anyway; AD items stay on Ashe, AP carries carry
- **No Zac yet:** Annie / Renekton hold tank items; 5 different Beach Pop / Juggernaut at 7
|||
- **No Taliyah by 5-1:** Ashe stays, Syndra keeps AP items; put any 5-cost next to an instrument
- **No AD items:** everything AP on Syndra, later Taliyah
- **Front line melts:** Gargoyle on Zac, Swain for Yuumi
:::

::: box gold
### 🏆 Top Cut (lobbies of 8 · top 4 advance · 1 game per round)
- **4th = 1st, 5th = out.** Roll at 3-2 if HP is under 60 · solid board at 8 before greeding 9 · combat augments over econ after 3-2
- Count who's weaker than you in stage 4: 4+ below → protect HP; fewer → spend now. **In the final, play for 1st.**
